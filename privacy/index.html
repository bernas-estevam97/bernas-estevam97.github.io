Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data is collected when you use this website, why it is processed, where it is sent, and how you can exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for personal data processed through this website is:

Bernardo Estevam
PhD candidate in Biomedical Sciences, University of Algarve
Location: Portugal, European Union
Enquiries: via the contact form or on LinkedIn.

2. What data I collect

This website follows the principle of data minimisation. Personal data is collected only when you choose to submit the contact form:

  • Your name: used to address you in my reply.
  • Your email address: used to reply to your message.
  • Your message: any details you write in the message field.
  • Anti-spam verification data: when you submit the form, a captcha token generated by hCaptcha is processed temporarily to confirm the submission comes from a person rather than an automated script.

No tracking or profiling: this website does not use tracking cookies, analytics, marketing pixels or fingerprinting. Browsing it is anonymous.

3. Why I collect it

Data sent through the contact form is processed only to:

  • Read and respond to your message.
  • Discuss research collaborations, academic enquiries or small side projects.
  • Protect the website against automated spam, abuse and security threats.

Your data is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers, and it is never added to marketing lists or newsletters.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under the GDPR, processing is based on:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): by filling in the contact form and pressing "Send message", you consent to your details being transmitted and processed to answer your message.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): processing is necessary to protect the website against automated abuse and to manage correspondence.

5. Where it is stored and processed

Form submissions are not stored in a database on this website. When you submit the form:

  • The data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • It is sent through Web3Forms, which delivers it directly to my personal Google Mail inbox.
  • The message is stored in my Google Mail account (Google LLC), protected by two-factor authentication and operating under European Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

6. Third-party services

The following external processors are used to deliver messages reliably and without spam:

  • Web3Forms: form endpoint that receives the submission over HTTPS and routes it to email. Web3Forms does not sell personal data or keep submission content beyond routing.
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.): anti-bot verification that analyses technical signals, such as mouse movement or browser headers, to tell whether the interaction is human (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Google Workspace / Gmail (Google Ireland Limited): email service where incoming messages are stored and read.

7. How long I keep your data

Messages and contact details are kept only as long as needed for the purpose of your message:

  • General enquiries are kept for the duration of the conversation and deleted once the matter is resolved.
  • If the conversation leads to an ongoing collaboration, correspondence may be kept for documentation while the project runs.
  • You can ask for your messages to be deleted at any time.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Art. 15): request a copy of any personal data I hold about you.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): request that I permanently delete your messages and personal data.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): limit how your data is used under certain legal conditions.
  • Portability (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection (Art. 21): object at any time to the processing of your personal data.

9. How to make a request

To ask a question about this policy, exercise any of your rights, or have your messages deleted:

  • Contact form: send a short note through the contact form with "Privacy request" in the message.
  • LinkedIn: message me at linkedin.com/in/bernas-estevam97.

Requests are answered without undue delay and at the latest within 30 calendar days, as required by the GDPR.
