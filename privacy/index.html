Data Protection

Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

I value your privacy and believe in complete transparency. This policy explains what personal data is collected when you interact with this website, why it is processed, where it is sent, and how you can exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for personal data processed through this website is:

Bernardo Estevam
Bioinformatician, Automation Developer & Data Analyst
Location: Portugal, European Union
Direct inquiries: via the Website Contact Form or on LinkedIn.

2. What Data I Collect

This website operates on the principle of strict data minimization. I only collect personal data when you voluntarily choose to provide it by submitting the contact form:

  • Your Name: Used to address you respectfully in subsequent correspondence.
  • Your Email Address: Used to reply to your inquiry or message.
  • Your Message: Any details, project requests, or information you write in the message textarea.
  • Anti-Spam Verification Data: When submitting the contact form, an interactive captcha token generated by hCaptcha is processed temporarily to verify that the submission is from a human rather than an automated script.

No Tracking or Profiling: This website does not use tracking cookies, Google Analytics, marketing pixels, or user fingerprinting technologies. Browsing the portfolio is completely anonymous.

3. Why I Collect It (Purpose of Processing)

The personal information you transmit through the contact form is processed solely for the following explicit purposes:

  • To read, evaluate, and respond to your communication, feedback, or business inquiries.
  • To discuss potential professional engagements, bioinformatics research collaborations, or employment opportunities.
  • To protect the website against automated spam submissions, abuse, and security threats.

Your data is never sold, rented, leased, or shared with third-party advertisers, and it will never be added to marketing lists or newsletters.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), processing is based on:

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): When you fill in the contact form and press "Send Message", you give explicit consent for your details to be transmitted and processed for the purpose of answering your inquiry.
  • Legitimate Interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing is necessary to protect the website infrastructure against malicious bot abuse (anti-spam) and to manage professional correspondence effectively.

5. Where It Is Stored and Processed

This website does not store form submissions in a public database. Instead, when you click submit:

  • The form data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • It is transmitted securely via Web3Forms, which packages the submission and delivers it directly to my personal Google Mail / Workspace inbox.
  • Your message is stored securely within my authenticated Google Mail account (Google LLC), safeguarded by two-factor authentication and Google's industry-standard enterprise security practices, operating under European Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

6. Third-Party Services & Sub-processors

To ensure reliable, spam-free message delivery, the following external processors are utilized:

  • Web3Forms: Form endpoint service that receives the contact form submission via HTTPS and routes it directly to email. Web3Forms does not sell personal data or retain submission content beyond routing.
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.): Anti-bot and anti-spam verification. hCaptcha analyzes technical signals (such as mouse movements or browser headers) to determine if the interaction is human, compliant with GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).
  • Google Workspace / Gmail (Google Ireland Limited): Secure cloud email service where incoming messages are stored and read.

7. How Long I Retain Your Data

I retain your messages and contact details only for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfill the original purpose of your communication:

  • General inquiries are kept for the duration of the conversation and deleted once the matter is resolved.
  • If our discussion leads to an ongoing collaborative or professional relationship, correspondence may be retained for documentation purposes during the course of the project.
  • You may request the deletion of your messages at any time.

8. Your Rights Under EU GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, EU citizens and residents possess significant rights concerning their personal data:

  • Right to Access (Art. 15): You have the right to request a copy of any personal data I hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): You may request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): You have the right to request that I permanently delete your emails and personal data from my records.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): You have the right to limit how your data is used under certain legal conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): You may request to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): You may object at any time to the processing of your personal information.

9. How to Request Access, Correction, or Deletion

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise any of your data protection rights, or would like your sent messages deleted, please reach out directly:

  • Via the Contact Form: Send a brief note on the contact form with the subject line "Privacy Request".
  • Via LinkedIn: Reach out directly on my verified profile at linkedin.com/in/bernas-estevam97.

Requests will be acknowledged and processed without undue delay, and at the latest within 30 calendar days as required by GDPR.
