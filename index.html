About

I study motor behaviour in rodent models through kinematic analysis, and write the software that turns raw tracking and imaging data into reproducible results.

My doctoral work at the Faculty of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences focuses on kinematic motor analysis, tracking algorithms, biomechanical modelling and machine learning. Before the PhD, I developed research applications at the Algarve Biomedical Center that replaced slow manual analysis steps.

Currently

Focused on my doctoral research and not looking for new positions. Open to research collaborations, questions about my tools and occasional small side projects.

Research software

Tools and databases I have built for neuroscience and biomedical research.

Web & database

PolyQ Database

A Django web application backed by Microsoft SQL Server that hosts clinical and research data for the nine known polyglutamine neurodegenerative disorders, from molecular pathology to disease genetics.

  • Python/Django
  • MSSQL
  • JavaScript
Visit polyq.pt
Web application

Mouseframe

Processes digitised images of rodent footprint patterns and extracts quantitative gait measurements for locomotion research.

  • Django
  • JavaScript
  • SQLite
Visit mouseframe.pt
Desktop app

Mouseframe Desktop

Electron counterpart to Mouseframe for offline gait image analysis with local caching and batch import.

  • Electron
